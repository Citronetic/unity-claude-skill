Did you know 50% of self-proclaimed game developers on Reddit have never released a game? To me that is crazy! Nothing can teach you faster than sticking with a project and releasing it to the world, even a super small one. By far the biggest reason for this is their first game idea is too big. In this lesson, I go over some tips on bringing that ambitious game idea down to reality.
