## 1. Overview

Q&A (0)

Last time, you added cameras to your guided project scene to control what the user can see and what they should be looking at.

Post-processing allows you to apply cinematic effects to your cameras and create the visual language that users are used to seeing in film and games.

In this tutorial, you’ll add post-processing effects to your guided project.
