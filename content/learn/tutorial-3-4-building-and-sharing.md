## 1. Enter the app’s name and version number

Q&A (0)

First, you want to check that the name, developer/company, and thumbnail aren’t the default placeholders.

**1.  Access the Player Settings:**

- Click **Edit** > **Project Settings**,
- On the left side, select the **Player** panel.

**2.  Change the default app information:**

- Change the **Company Name, Product Name**, and **Version** **Number** to reflect the current state of your app.

**3.  If you want to edit the Splash Image that appears when the app starts up:**

- Expand the **Splash Image**fold-out and edit its settings.
- You will need at least a Pro license to disable the Splash Image.

[Image: image]

Your App should now display on your device with its proper name, and should load with the splash image you want.

**Related resources:**

- [Naming Best Practices](https://developer.oculus.com/distribute/publish-content-bp/)
- [Icon Best Practices](https://developer.oculus.com/distribute/asset-guidelines/)

## 2. Confirm recommended publish settings

Q&A (0)

In addition to optimizing the performance of your app, generally, there are also a few platform-specific recommendations that will make your app build and perform at its best.

Since specific recommendations are platform-specific and are changing all the time, the best way to find up-to-date and accurate information is searching online.

**1.  Find guidance on recommended settings for VR publication:**

- Explore the links under “Related Resources” below to find specific recommendations.
- Google “Recommended Unity settings for [insert_device]”.

**2.  Configure Player Settings:**

- Click Edit > Project Settings, then click **Player** from the left panel.
- Scroll down to the **Other Settings** fold-out.

**3.  Configure Quality settings:**

- Click **Quality** from the left panel of Project Settings.

**4.  Configure XR settings:**

- Click **XR Plug-in Management**from the left panel of Project Settings.

**5.  Configure rendering settings:**

- Locate and select the **UniversalRenderPipelineAsset**from the Project window.

**6.  Confirm shadow settings:**

- For any **Realtime or Mixed Lights**, ensure you are using appropriate shadows.

**7.  To confirm Build Settings:**

- Click File > Build Settings.

[Image: image]

Your app should now abide by all of the recommended settings for publishing to your target platform.

**Related resources**:

- [Configuration Settings](https://developer.oculus.com/documentation/unity/unity-conf-settings/)
- [Blog: Unity Settings for Mobile VR](https://developer.oculus.com/blog/tech-note-unity-settings-for-mobile-vr/?locale=en_US)
- [Stereo Rendering in VR / AR](https://blogs.unity3d.com/2017/11/21/how-to-maximize-ar-and-vr-performance-with-advanced-stereo-rendering/?ampDeviceId=53ad3448-c94c-4a0d-abf9-666d0f82aae1&ampSessionId=1774863089977&ampTimestamp=1774949575382)

## 3. Look into sharing or publishing your app

Q&A (0)

Up to this point, you have only been building and running the app through Unity. Take a look at what it would take to distribute your app outside of Unity.

**1.  Explore the requirements for submitting your app to an official store:**

- Use the links below to read more about the process.
- Google “Submit app to [insert_store_name]” (e.g. Steam, Oculus Store).

**2.  Explore alternative platforms for distributing your App:**

- Explore requirements for submitting your app to sites like [SideQuest](https://sidequestvr.com/listing-guide) and [Itch.io](https://itch.io/games/tag-virtual-reality).

You should now have a sense of the various platforms available for distributing your app and an understanding of what it might take to have an app approved for one of those platforms.

**Related resources:**

- [Submit your app to the Oculus Store](https://developer.oculus.com/distribute/)
- [Lifecycle of an Oculus App](https://developer.oculus.com/distribute/publish-app-review/)
- [Oculus Dev Documentation](https://developer.oculus.com/documentation/unity/)
- [Oculus VRCs](https://developer.oculus.com/distribute/publish-quest-req/)

## 5. Extension Activities

Q&A (0)

If you want to further develop your skills, explore new concepts, and improve your project, check out some of the optional extension activities below.

Each one is tagged as [Easy], [Medium], [Difficult], or [Expert] and will also include a [Requires Programming] tag if some coding is required.

**1.  Research the publication process [Easy]**

Research the requirements for submitting an app to your preferred platform:

- This could include an official app marketplace like the [Oculus Store](https://www.oculus.com/experiences) or [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/).
- You could also submit your app to an unofficial platform like [SideQuest](https://sidequestvr.com/) or [Itch.io](https://itch.io/).

**2.  Create some artwork for your app [Medium]**

Take epic screenshots, make an app icon, hero image, or other materials to showcase your app:

- You can use the [Unity Recorder](https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/com.unity.recorder@2.0/manual/index.html?ampDeviceId=53ad3448-c94c-4a0d-abf9-666d0f82aae1&ampSessionId=1774863089977&ampTimestamp=1774949575382) to capture high quality images of your app.
- Follow guidelines from your relevant app platform on how to make the best icon or imagery

- E.g. Oculus [Blog on How to Prep App for Submission](https://developer.oculus.com/blog/how-to-prep-your-app-for-submission/?locale=en_US)
- E.g. Sidequest [requirements on image sizes](https://sidequestvr.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/SIDEQUEST/pages/196610/How+To+Submit+an+App+to+SideQuest) for banner images and icons
