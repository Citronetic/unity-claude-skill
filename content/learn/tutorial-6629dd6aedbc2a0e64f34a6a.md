## 1. Choosing selection units

Q&A (0)

When the **ProBuilder** window is open in the Editor, four toggle buttons at the top of the **Scene** view appear.

[Image: image]

These are divided by the selection units (**object**,**vertex**,**line**, and**surface**) for 3D model editing. Other 3D modeling tools have similar features as well.

[Image: image]

To see how the selection is made, make a basic or custom shape and change the selection units

## 2. Freely arrange components

Q&A (0)

As you have probably noticed, gizmos, which appear when you select a GameObject, appear when you select vertices, lines, or surfaces as well. This means you can also select and arrange the vertices, lines, and surfaces.

[Image: image]

The gizmos follow the basic tools of the scene, so use the tools to arrange them however you want. For detailed arrangement, turn off the snap you previously turned on or hold down the Ctrl key when you work to temporarily disable it.

[Image: image]Mark step as complete

## 3. Adjusting size

Q&A (0)

GameObjects in Unity have the **Transform** component, which impacts the size, position, and rotation of the GameObject. You can increase or decrease the size by adjusting the **Scale**properties.

[Image: image]

A GameObject whose position and rotation has been changed retains its own shape while maintaining the size, whereas a GameObject whose scale has been increased has its size expanded. Typically, when working on 3D modeling, shapes are built with the scale set to **1**by default.

Mark step as complete

## 4. Modeling by copying surfaces

Q&A (0)

Hold the **Shift**key while selecting surfaces among the components to copy them.

[Image: image]

When you move or change the size of an edge or face while holding down the **Shift**key, the connected surfaces as well as the selected ones are copied together, forming a new shape from the original one. You can use this technique to make more complex shapes.
