**Description**

This session brings together a diverse lineup of industry experts, including creative directors, narrative directors, game designers, and cinematic directors.

**The panelists**

😎 Emily Grace Buck is a renowned narrative designer with credits on over a dozen video games. Currently the Studio Narrative Director at EA DICE, she leads the narrative for the next Battlefield project and Battlefield: 2042 live service. Emily's diverse background includes roles at Ubisoft Reflections, Gato Studio, Eko, and Lucid Games. Best known for her work at Telltale Games, her credits include The Walking Dead: The Final Season, Batman: The Enemy Within, and Marvel's Guardians of the Galaxy. With experience in interactive television, training simulations, tabletop RPGs, and acting, Emily brings a wealth of expertise to the world of video game storytelling.

😎 Neel Upadhye spent 14 years at EA and while there, he was a  director on the narrative team of Battlefield 2042,  served as lead writer on Need for Speed Unbound. Neel created the series “Stories from the Outlands” for the billion dollar gaming franchise Apex Legends, and had the joy of directing multiple internet-breaking shorts. Most recently, he led a studio of 50+ in the production of an original ARPG at Azra Games as VP of Creative Development.

Prior to EA and Games, Neel worked in Film and TV. His credits include a feature film on Amazon Prime, a TV pilot for Disney XD, and two Star Wars shorts produced by ILM, including the award-winning "Hunted" for Star Wars: Squadrons.

😎 Kevin Leung - from Castlevania all the way to Hades, Kevin Leung wanted nothing more than to make video games. Graduated from the University of Waterloo with a degree in mathematics and cognitive psychology, Kevin draws from 20 years of experience, taking on many roles in the disciplines of Level, Game, and Narrative Design. A selection of AAA titles from his gameography include Metal Gear Solid: The Twin Snakes, Rainbow Six: Vegas, with the latest coming soon entitled Unknown 9: Awakening . His first endeavor as a bonafide storyteller and scriptwriter was Catan Stories: Legend of the Sea Robbers. He currently lives in Montreal, Canada.

😎 Dori Arazi is an Award Winning Cinematic Director with a passion for visual storytelling. Working with talent on-set, he combines a keen eye to foster strong performance with evocative imagery and creative shot designs. Dori is a Virtual Production creative and technical trailblazer, designing custom equipment and pipelines. Combined with a deep and intuitive grasp of an array of cinematic languages, Dori sets new quality bars from previs to content finalization.

​Dori's broad background in animation, VFX and game development allows him to bring forward a unified, cross-disciplinary vision throughout production. Most known for his award winning work on "God of War", he has built and directed cinematic teams for: Sony, EA, Midway Games, THQ and SEGA.. Dori founded and leads the Society for Game Cinematics. He is based in CA, US

**Our moderator**

😎 Andrea Saravia Pérez , a multilingual narrative designer and producer, brings 5+ years of experience from AAA video game companies to her role as co-founder of Small Loan Studio, an independent game accelerator. With a passion for storytelling,  a diverse skill set, and in true indie fashion, Andrea has taken on various roles within the studio, including creative director, lead sound designer, and lead producer, contributing to the development of multiple indie titles. Her expertise and leadership skills have been instrumental in guiding the studio's projects from concept to completion.  Born in Colombia, Andrea resides and works in the US.
