## 1. Setting up Reverb Zones

Q&A (0)

- Create a new Audio Reverb Zone  **( Create > Audio > AudioReverbZone)**named AudioReverbZone_Underpass and position and scale it under the walkway in the scene.

[Image: image]

- With the newly created GameObject selected, change the Reverb Preset to Cave to create an echo type sound for the underpass area.

[Image: image]

- Create another AudioReverb Zone named AudioReverbZone_Underwater and position and scale it in the roundabout fountain.

[Image: image]

- Configure the Audio Reverb Zone to use the Underwater Reverb Preset.

[Image: image]

- Test out these areas in play mode by walking into the Reverb Zones.
