[Image: Project Files - Create Real-Time Visualizations with Unity Reflect and Unity Pro]Summary

In this workshop, participants will create a Realtime 3D (RT3D) visualization in Unity with the power of Reflect. This workshop is designed for users who have little to no familiarity with Unity and will cover foundational topics needed to begin developing projects in the engine. Participants will begin by preparing Reflect for development by configuring their project and setting it up for use in Revit. Using Reflect’s one click solution, participants will send their models into the Unity editor for further development.
