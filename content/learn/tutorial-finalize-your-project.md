## 4. Consider your next steps with Unity

Q&A (0)

After you’ve finished your project and maybe even passed the exam, what will you do next? Consider some of the options below:

#### Solidify your programming fundamentals

If you’re not already confident with your programming, it’s recommended that you start with the [**Junior Programmer** pathway](https://learn.unity.com/pathway/junior-programmer), which will give you the foundational skills needed to jump-start any future C# development with Unity.

#### Consider AR or VR development

You’ve made one game, but have only really focused on the desktop platform. Consider expanding your knowledge into extended reality (XR). Unity also has a [**VR development** pathway](https://learn.unity.com/pathway/vr-development) and an [**AR development** pathway](https://learn.unity.com/pathway/mobile-ar-development) that will help you get started.

#### Continue with more game development

If you want to continue down the games path, Unity Learn has a wide range of games-based learning content, including the following:

- [3D Stealth Game: Haunted House](https://learn.unity.com/course/3d-stealth-game-haunted-house)
- [2D Adventure Game: Robot Repair](https://learn.unity.com/course/691f277138cfaaa01c448657)
