## 1. Locate and Download the Course Materials

Q&A (0)

- On the main project page, click on the Course Materials tab, located behind the Overview tab **(figure 01)**

[Image: image]

- Click on *UCP Exam Prep Workshop Assets.zip*
- Download the files to someplace easy to remember, such as your desktop.

The asset files are divided into sections based on their chapter and activity. Use the start files to work on the assigned challenge and the complete files to review the solutions as needed.
