THINK LIKE A DIRECTOR!  Or, how I’ve applied cinematic lessons learned from 16 years in the Pixar Art Department towards creating deeply engaging visual-narrative experiences across different media.

This talk provides a design-centric approach balancing Story, Visual and Emotional design for creating visual narratives that are moving, memorable and meaningful.

This talk is for anyone who enjoys creating and or making games, films, animation, comics, picture books and more.

Take away a greater understanding and appreciation for the art and craft of visual-emotional storytelling that will inspire and enrich you as both a consumer and creator of storytelling experiences.

Here are a sample of topics to be covered:

🎦  What is Design?  How does Design relate to Style in terms of cinematic visual storytelling?

🎦  Setting the stage--How environments convey character. 📽

🎦 Designing Feeling  -- How shape and color design convey an emotional landscape.

🎦 Power is Relative  -- How compositional relationship of character to surroundings conveys power dynamics.
