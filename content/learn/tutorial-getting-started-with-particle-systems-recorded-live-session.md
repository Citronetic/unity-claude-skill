[Image: Recorded Live Session - Getting started with Particle Systems ]Summary

Watch the recording of the Learn Live virtual session where a Unity Certified Instructor led learners through a series of mini-challenges to  make fire, smoke, snow, and rain using Unity’s Particle System. By the end of this session you will:

- Make fire effects with Unity’s Particle System
- Make rain & snow effects with Unity’s Particle System
- Customize the particle shapes with custom 2D textures.
- Modify the omission shape, color, lifespan, and other properties to customize the Particle System’s shape.
- Time the Particle System at specific time stamped moments.
- Understand the utility of the deterministic nature, from a cinematography perspective.
