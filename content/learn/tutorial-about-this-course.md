## 1. Overview

Q&A (0)

Welcome to Growing your mobile game.

In this course, you'll develop a comprehensive understanding of the core concepts of monetization, user acquisition and revenue strategies. The course has five sections:

- Welcome to the course
- Monetizing your game
- Acquiring your users
- Strategizing for success
- Next steps

Throughout the course, you'll have the opportunity to reflect on the best options for your game through real examples, case studies, quizzes and video interviews with Unity monetization experts.

Real case studies and links to third parties have been used in this course. This is to help you with current practical tips and to keep the agnostic nature of the course, not with the intent to endorse any products or services.

The information in this course is meant to supplement, not replace, appropriate business and marketing training.

## 2. Who is this course for?

Q&A (0)

This course provides the foundational monetization and user acquisition knowledge needed to grow a  free-to-play mobile game.

The course is aimed at:

- Developers who are potentially responsible for a small studio which may or may not have released a game.
- Developers who are in the process of developing a game and would like to start planning their strategy ahead.
- Developers who have recently launched a game for the first time and would like to review their efforts at a foundational level.

The course assumes no previous knowledge or experience in monetization and user acquisition.

## 3. The structure of the course

Q&A (0)

#### Welcome to the course

This introductory section will introduce you to how to use this course and the monetization and user acquisition foundations.

After this, you will explore monetization, user acquisition and how to strategize for success.

#### Monetizing your game

In this project, you’ll explore how to monetize your game, from types of monetization through to how to select and measure efficacy of monetization solution providers.

#### Acquiring your users

In this project, you’ll explore different marketing channels such as organic and paid efforts, how to select different types of user acquisition, how to implement performance-driven user acquisition, and how to measure results.

#### Strategizing for success

In this project, you’ll explore how to create a successful monetization and user acquisition strategy, how to gather, consolidate and interpret data, and how to iterate your strategy.

#### Next steps

This tutorial will provide a summary of the key insights and practical next steps to keep growing your game.
