[Image: DOTS Fundamentals]Summary

This course explores the principles that underpin Unity’s Data-Oriented Technology Stack (DOTS) and data-oriented design. In this workshop, participants will be introduced to the key concepts of DOTS and will learn how to start working with DOTS, specifically Jobs and the ECS. Participants will learn how to set up Unity for DOTS and get hands-on experience with DOTS by applying the fundamentals of ECS in the editor. By the end of this workshop, participants will be prepared to start working with DOTS in the Unity Editor.
