**Create with Code - Teacher Training - Section 2 of 3**

In this section of the Create with Code Teacher Training, you will take the time required to configure the course in a way that best suits your needs and the requirements of your classroom. First, you will review common course configurations, providing some context for the flexibility that is possible. Next, you will examine your own class' situation, including its unique affordances and constraints. Finally, you will choose a course configuration that works best for you, including which (if any) parts of the course you will exclude because of time limitations.
