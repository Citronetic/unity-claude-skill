[Image: Pixyz to Substance Painter to Unity]Summary

In this tutorial, we’ll simulate being an engineer/artist with a tight deadline. In this scenario, my boss has asked me to take a new CAD design and get it into an attractive interactive experience in the next few hours. This tutorial demonstrates how to approach the process, including ingestion, tessellation, optimization, masking, painting, baking, exporting textures and materials, adding lighting and HDRP in Unity, and using Unity AI Muse to create C# scripts for me to use.
