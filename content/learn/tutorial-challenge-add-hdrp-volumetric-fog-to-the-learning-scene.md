## 4. Define your evaluation criteria

Q&A (0)

Before you implement Volumetric Fog in the learning Scene, consider how you will evaluate its success at creating a mysterious atmosphere. You can do this by setting clear evaluation criteria, and using these as a guide when assessing your work in progress.

One useful basis for evaluation criteria is the [four domains of high visual fidelity](https://learn.unity.com/tutorial/introduction-to-high-visual-fidelity?projectId=5d78f84eedbc2a0020956e88#5da433ecedbc2a0023e0533e), for example:

- Does the Volumetric Fog implementation match the creative vision established in the Buried Memories: Serekh concept art? (Scope)
- Would the implementation increase the player’s sense of immersion and make a positive impact on game feel? (Performance)
- Does the implementation combine cohesively with lighting and any effects in the Scene? (Polish)

You can also ask a friend or colleague to help you evaluate your performance against the criteria you choose. It can be helpful to get an outside perspective when evaluating creative work and considering the perspectives of different potential players.
