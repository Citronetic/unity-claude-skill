[Image: 9.  How did I stay motivated to finish The First Tree?]Summary

In this case study, I talk about one of the biggest problems facing indie devs: losing motivation on your project. I talk about the importance of treating your motivation like a depletable resource like time and money, and how to maximize that motivation. I also talk about some simple tips that can help you make it to the finish line.
