In this tutorial, you’ll learn the ins and outs of the gaming growth industry. There are two sides to game growth: user acquisition and monetization. Throughout this video series, you’ll learn about the key technologies that help to fuel game growth, the fundamentals of monetization for games, and how to acquire users through ads.

In this video, you will learn:

- The most used growth technologies and platforms
