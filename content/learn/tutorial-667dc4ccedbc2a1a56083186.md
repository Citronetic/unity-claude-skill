[Image: Growing with the offerwall]Summary

In this tutorial, you’ll learn how to optimize your monetization and UA campaigns with offerwall. An offerwall is an in-app marketplace that works like a mini-store, listing multiple offers users can complete in exchange for in-app rewards or bonuses. Throughout this video series, you’ll learn the basics of creating an offerwall, placement strategy, how to set up an offerwall strategy, and how to grow monetization and UA offers. By the end of this series, you’ll have the tools to build an offerwall campaign and scale it up - so you can boost your revenue, engagement, completions, and more.
