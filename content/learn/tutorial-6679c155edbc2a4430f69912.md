## 1. Impact goals and measurements

Q&A (0)

As a creator of games for impact, your work has the potential to make meaningful, positive change in the world. By aligning your game mechanics with your impact goals, you can design experiences that engage players while promoting awareness, shifting mindsets, and even influencing real-world behaviors.

To maximize the impact of your game, it’s essential to keep your impact goals at the forefront of your mind throughout the development process. Establish a clear vision for the change you want to create and allow that vision to guide your design decisions. Regularly iterate and playtest to ensure that your game's mechanics effectively reinforce your intended message. Engage with experts and stakeholders who can provide valuable insights and help amplify the reach and influence of your completed game.

For example, in grantee [Crab God](https://www.chaostheorygames.com/work/crab-god), by Chaos Theory Games, the development team shared how their game aligns to specific SDG goals.

[Image: image]

Measuring the outcomes of your game, both qualitatively and quantitatively, is crucial for assessing its effectiveness in achieving your desired impact. By sharing your findings with the broader game development community, you contribute to the collective understanding of how games can be harnessed as a powerful force for good.

Above all, stay connected with your fellow creators who share your passion for making a difference through their work. Engage with communities like Unity for Humanity, participate in game jams, and support and uplift one another. Together, we can leverage the unique strengths of the gaming medium to address important issues, expand perspectives, and drive positive change in the world, one game at a time.
