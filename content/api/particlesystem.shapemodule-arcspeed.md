                    public [ParticleSystem.MinMaxCurve](ParticleSystem.MinMaxCurve.html) arcSpeed;






### Description



In animated modes, this determines how quickly the particle emission position moves around the arc.





The value is specified in terms of the number of complete 360 degree rotations. A value of 2 would mean that the particle emission position rotated 2 full times per second.
Additional resources: [ParticleSystem.ShapeModule.arcMode](ParticleSystem.ShapeModule-arcMode.html), [ParticleSystemShapeMultiModeValue](ParticleSystemShapeMultiModeValue.html), [ParticleSystem.ShapeModule.shapeType](ParticleSystem.ShapeModule-shapeType.html).
