### Description



The unit of illuminance (lumen per square meter).





A light source that emits 1 lumen of luminous flux onto an area of 1 square meter has an illuminance of 1 lux.

Compatible light types: [LightType.Directional](LightType.Directional.html), [LightType.Box](LightType.Box.html), [LightType.Point](LightType.Point.html), [LightType.Spot](LightType.Spot.html), [LightType.Pyramid](LightType.Pyramid.html).

Additional resources: [Light.lightUnit](Light-lightUnit.html), [light component](../Manual/class-Light.html).
