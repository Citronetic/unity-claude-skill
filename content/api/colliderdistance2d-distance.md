                    public float distance;






### Description



Gets the distance between two colliders.





The distance between two colliders is defined as the length of a [Vector2](Vector2.html) between [pointA](ColliderDistance2D-pointA.html) and [pointB](ColliderDistance2D-pointB.html).  The [distance](ColliderDistance2D-distance.html) between these points can be positive indicating that the [Collider2D](Collider2D.html) are separated (not overlapped), zero indicating that they are touching (but not overlapped) or negative indicating that they are overlapped.
