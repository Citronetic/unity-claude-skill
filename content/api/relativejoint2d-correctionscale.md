                    public float correctionScale;






### Description



Scales both the linear and angular forces used to correct the required relative orientation.





Adjusting the correction scale will scale the amount of force and torque applied by the joint. No more than the specified [maxForce](RelativeJoint2D-maxForce.html) or [maxTorque](RelativeJoint2D-maxTorque.html) will be applied however.

Additional resources: [maxForce](RelativeJoint2D-maxForce.html) or [maxTorque](RelativeJoint2D-maxTorque.html).
