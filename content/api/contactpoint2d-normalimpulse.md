                    public float normalImpulse;






### Description



Gets the impulse applied at the contact point along the [ContactPoint2D.normal](ContactPoint2D-normal.html).





The impulse is applied to both the [rigidbody](ContactPoint2D-rigidbody.html) and the [otherRigidbody](ContactPoint2D-otherRigidbody.html) at the [point](ContactPoint2D-point.html) along the [normal](ContactPoint2D-normal.html) and the inverse [normal](ContactPoint2D-normal.html).
