                    public float transferProgress;






### Description



The transfering progress of the downloaded Android asset pack.





A floating point value. The range is 0 through 1.
Read-only.
Asset packs get compressed to reduce the download size. After they are downloaded, they have to be uncompressed.
Additional resources: [AndroidAssetPacks.DownloadAssetPackAsync](Android.AndroidAssetPacks.DownloadAssetPackAsync.html).
