### Description



The different areas of profiling, corresponding to the charts in [ProfilerWindow](ProfilerWindow.html).





Each area corresponds to a chart in [ProfilerWindow](ProfilerWindow.html) and the accompanying detail pane in its bottom half. These are the categories that group statistical data and provide additional insights into those specific areas.
