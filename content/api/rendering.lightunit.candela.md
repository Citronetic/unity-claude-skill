### Description



The base unit of luminous intensity in the International System of Units.





For reference, a common wax candle emits light with a luminous intensity of roughly 1 candela.

Compatible light types: [LightType.Point](LightType.Point.html), [LightType.Spot](LightType.Spot.html), [LightType.Pyramid](LightType.Pyramid.html).

Additional resources: [Light.lightUnit](Light-lightUnit.html), [light component](../Manual/class-Light.html).
