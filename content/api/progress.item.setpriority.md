### Description



Sets the progress indicator's priority.





Progress indicators are sorted by priority, from highest to lowest. Priorities also determine the visual behavior of progress indicators (see [Priority](Progress.Priority.html)).

Additional resources: [Progress.Item.priority](Progress.Item-priority.html), [Progress.SetPriority](Progress.SetPriority.html), [Progress.GetPriority](Progress.GetPriority.html).
