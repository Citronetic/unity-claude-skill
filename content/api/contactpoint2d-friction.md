                    public float friction;






### Description



The effective friction used for the [ContactPoint2D](ContactPoint2D.html).





When two [Collider2D](Collider2D.html) come into contact, their [Collider2D.friction](Collider2D-friction.html) values combine.

This property represents the combined friction and is used by the physics solver to calculate the collision response.

Additional resources: [PhysicsMaterial2D.friction](PhysicsMaterial2D-friction.html), [ContactPoint2D.bounciness](ContactPoint2D-bounciness.html)
