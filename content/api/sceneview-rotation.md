### Description



The direction of the [camera](SceneView-camera.html) to the [pivot](SceneView-pivot.html) of the [SceneView](SceneView.html).





Changes the Scene view direction. Use with [pivot](SceneView-pivot.html), [size](SceneView-size.html), and [CameraSettings.fieldOfView](SceneView.CameraSettings-fieldOfView.html)  to calculate camera transformation. Use the camera [LookAt](SceneView.LookAt.html) to animate the transition.
