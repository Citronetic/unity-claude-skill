                    public float frequency;






### Description



The frequency at which the spring oscillates around the distance between the objects.





The spring will oscillate with a certain frequency as it attempts to bring the two anchor points of the objects together.  A [dampingRatio](FixedJoint2D-dampingRatio.html)' can be set to cause this oscillation to die down over time.

Additional resources: [dampingRatio](FixedJoint2D-dampingRatio.html).
