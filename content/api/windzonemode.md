### Description



Modes a Wind Zone can have, either Spherical or Directional.





You can have more than one Spherical Wind Zone in a Scene, but it does not make much
sense to have more than one Directional Wind Zone in your Scene as it affects
the whole Scene. This Wind Zone Mode is used by the [WindZone.mode](WindZone-mode.html) member.





### Properties



| Property | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Directional | Wind zone affects the entire Scene in one direction. |
| Spherical | Wind zone only has an effect inside the radius, and has a falloff from the center towards the edge. |
