# Embedded systems

Embedded systems are specialized platforms designed for hardware products where the software supports the functionality of the device.

The Unity Editor for Embedded Platforms is available under separate terms. For download information and licensing, contact your Account Manager or the [Unity Sales](https://create.unity.com/unity-for-industries?sfcid=7015G000000KFqdQAG&sflsa=2023-04-na-dg-hmi-solutions-contact-us&ampDeviceId=fffb83ff-ec04-401c-8bb9-ed5ebd99cd33&ampSessionId=1774849551466&ampTimestamp=1774936928584) team.
