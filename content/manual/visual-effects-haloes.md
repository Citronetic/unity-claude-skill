# Light halos

Strategies for creating glowing halos around light sources, to give the impression of small dust particles in the air, and add atmosphere to your **scene**A Scene contains the environments and menus of your game. Think of each unique Scene file as a unique level. In each Scene, you place your environments, obstacles, and decorations, essentially designing and building your game in pieces. [More info](CreatingScenes.html)
