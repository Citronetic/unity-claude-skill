# Collect frame timing data

Use the `FrameTimingManager` API to collect data about the performance of individual frames in your application, and optionally display the data in the **Profiler**A window that helps you to optimize your game. It shows how much time is spent in the various areas of your game. For example, it can report the percentage of time spent rendering, animating, or in your game logic. [More info](Profiler.html)
