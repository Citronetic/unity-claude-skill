# Scenes

Scenes are where you work with content in Unity. Read the following information to find out more about **scenes**A Scene contains the environments and menus of your game. Think of each unique Scene file as a unique level. In each Scene, you place your environments, obstacles, and decorations, essentially designing and building your game in pieces. [More info](CreatingScenes.html)
