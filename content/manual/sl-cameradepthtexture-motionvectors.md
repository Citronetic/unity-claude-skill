# Output a motion vector texture from a camera

`DepthTextureMode.MotionVectors` builds a screen-sized RG16 (16-bit float/channel) texture, where screen space **pixel**The smallest unit in a computer image. Pixel size depends on your screen resolution. Pixel lighting is calculated at every screen pixel. [More info](ShadowPerformance.html)
See in [Glossary](Glossary.html#pixel) motion is encoded into the R&G channels. The pixel motion is encoded in screen UV space.
